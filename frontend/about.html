Our Mission

Revolutionizing Railway Station Luggage Mobility

Smart Porter is designed to empower both passengers and coolies across India. We bring modern ride-hailing principles to station luggage portering to ensure dignified wages for porters and effortless travel for passengers.

The Problem We Solve

Navigating railway stations with heavy bags, infant strollers, and parcels is physically strenuous. Unpredictable bargaining often causes friction between travelers and porters during tight train arrival windows.

The Smart Porter Solution

With Smart Porter, passengers book coolies with transparent tariffs ahead of time or right upon stepping out of their cab. Our algorithmic matching connects the booking with an available licensed coolie waiting at the respective platform or entrance.

Porter Welfare & Dignity
  • 90% Direct Share: 90% of the passenger fare directly reaches the coolie's digital wallet or cash earnings.
  • Equitable Work Dispatch: Automated round-robin & rating dispatch stops aggressive gate monopolies.
  • Police & IRCTC Verification: Ensures passenger safety and authentic license tracking.
